Privacy policy

This policy explains how One Turn Blocks, published by Ahmed GabAllah, handles information when you use the Android game. Questions may be sent to ahmed.uk.dev@gmail.com.

What the app stores locally

The app stores your current board and tray, score and best score, onboarding completion, sound and haptic preferences, trial start and trusted-time timestamps, and a cached Boolean indicating whether Google Play has confirmed the permanent unlock. This information remains in the app’s private storage on your device. It is used only to operate the game, restore progress and enforce access.

Google Play Billing

The app communicates with Google Play Billing to retrieve product information, start a purchase, acknowledge a completed purchase and check whether the permanent unlock is owned. Google Play processes the transaction. The developer does not directly receive or store your card details.

A purchase token is processed transiently by the app when required by Google Play Billing. It is not persisted or logged by One Turn Blocks. Google may process and retain purchase records and related identifiers under its own policies. A network connection is needed to purchase or restore ownership.

What the developer does not operate

One Turn Blocks has no advertising, analytics SDK, account system, custom backend, cloud synchronisation or developer-operated tracking. It does not request or collect location, contacts, messages, photos, videos, audio recordings, files, calendar data or the advertising identifier.

Backup and transfer

Automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app state are disabled. Progress, settings and trial data are therefore not transferred automatically to another installation or device. Permanent ownership is restored through Google Play when available.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Either action removes the local game, settings and trial records. It does not erase Google Play’s purchase record. A permanent purchase can be restored through Google Play after reinstalling.

Security and limitations

Local data is stored in Android’s private application storage, and app-originated cleartext traffic is disabled. No system can be guaranteed completely secure. The local trial is device-local and may reset if app data is cleared.

Children’s privacy

The game is not designed or marketed specifically for children under 13. It contains no account, social interaction, user-generated content or targeted advertising. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern may contact the email above.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be revised, and material changes will be reflected before the affected version is released.
