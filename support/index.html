Game help

Support

One Turn Blocks is an offline 8 × 8 block puzzle. Place all three tray pieces, complete rows or columns, and use one clockwise Turn during each set.

Trial and permanent unlock

The 24-hour trial does not start when you install or open the app. It starts after your first accepted block placement. When it ends, your board remains visible and permanent play is available through one Google Play purchase—never a subscription.

To restore, open Settings and choose Restore purchase. Google Play may be temporarily unavailable if the device is offline, Play services are unavailable, the signed-in account differs, or the app was not installed through a compatible Play testing or release track.

Settings and new games

Open Settings to switch sound or haptics on or off, replay How to Play, or start a new game. Starting a new game requires confirmation and does not restart the trial.

Saving, reinstalling and deleting data

Game progress, best score and preferences save automatically on this device. There is no cloud game-state synchronisation. Reinstalling or clearing storage removes local progress, preferences and trial records; a deleted local score cannot be recovered. A Google Play permanent purchase can be restored when Play is available.

To delete local data, open Android Settings, select Apps, choose One Turn Blocks, then Storage and clear the app’s storage. Menu wording varies by device.
